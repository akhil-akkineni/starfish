Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Yearly Rewind ("the app") is a personal project by Akhil Akkineni. It builds a private playlist of your top tracks in your own Spotify account. This page explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When you log in with Spotify, you are asked to grant these permissions:

  • Your top tracks (user-top-read): to pick the 20 tracks you've listened to most.
  • Your private playlists (playlist-read-private): only to check whether a "Yearly Rewind" playlist already exists.
  • Manage your private playlists (playlist-modify-private): to create the "Yearly Rewind" playlist and add or remove its tracks. No other playlist is changed.
  • Upload images (ugc-image-upload): to set the "Yearly Rewind" playlist's cover image.

What the app stores

  • The app keeps no database and does not save your Spotify data on its server. Your top tracks are read only while the playlist is being built.
  • The login token Spotify issues is kept in a signed session cookie in your browser so you stay logged in. It is removed when you log out or the session ends.
  • The app does not sell, share or analyse your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train machine-learning models.

Cookies

The app uses a single session cookie, strictly to keep you logged in. It uses no tracking or analytics cookies.

Disconnecting and deleting your data

  • Click Log out and disconnect in the app to remove the token from your browser.
  • To revoke the app's access completely, go to spotify.com/account/apps and choose Remove access for this app.
  • The "Yearly Rewind" playlist is yours. You can delete it in Spotify at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Spotify

The app uses the Spotify Web API. Spotify's handling of your data is covered by the Spotify Privacy Policy. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Spotify.
